Butler CAM Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

The short version: Butler CAM does not collect your data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and your projects stay yours.

Who we are

Butler CAM is made by Butler Makes LLC. This policy covers the Butler CAM app for Mac and iPad.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send your projects, drawings, pictures, settings or any other information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Your files. Projects, G-code and exported drawings are saved where you choose. If you choose a cloud folder such as iCloud Drive, that service stores the file under its own terms.
  • Files you open. The app reads only the drawings, pictures and projects you choose to open.
  • Your settings. Things like your bit library, recent files and appearance are stored by the app on your device.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have turned on sharing with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics. Apple provides these without identifying you. You can turn this off at any time in your device's privacy settings.

Children

Butler CAM does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
