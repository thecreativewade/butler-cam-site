Make your first sign

  1. Press New Job, type the size of your board, pick its wood and where zero is, then press Create.
  2. Draw a rectangle round the edge, then pick Aa and click to add your words.
  3. Pick the rectangle and press Cut Out. It cuts the sign out, with tabs to hold it.
  4. Pick the words and press V-Carve for sharp lettering, or Pocket to clear them out.
  5. Press 3D to watch it cut, then Save G-code and run the file on your machine.

Before you cut

  1. Clamp or screw the board down, away from where the bit will go.
  2. Fit the first bit, then set X0 Y0 and Z0 on the machine where the Save G-code tab says.
  3. If the job uses more than one bit, the machine pauses. Change the bit, set Z0 again, and press Resume.
  4. First time? Run it with Z set an inch high (an air cut) to check where it goes.

Always check a job before you run it and follow your machine's safety instructions. You are responsible for how your machine is set up and run.

Common questions

Which machines does it work with?

Butler CAM saves G-code for GRBL 1.1, grblHAL and machines that use standard G-code. Choose your machine when you save.

Does it control my CNC?

No. It makes a G-code file, and you run that file with your machine's own control software.

Which files can I bring in?

SVG and DXF drawings, and PNG, JPEG and HEIC pictures. You can also export your drawing as SVG or DXF.

How do I use a laser?

Pick shapes and press Laser Cut or Laser Fill, or press Laser Picture and choose a picture. Save G-code makes a separate laser file. On GRBL, run it with laser mode on ($32=1) and the laser focused on the top of the material.

Do my projects work on both Mac and iPad?

Yes. A project saved on one opens on the other. Move it by any means you like, such as iCloud Drive or AirDrop.

How do I use Apple Pencil?

Tap the hand at the top right. The Pencil then draws and your finger moves the view. Double-tap the Pencil to switch between your tool and Select.

Can I use it on iPhone?

Not at this time. Butler CAM is made for Mac and iPad.
